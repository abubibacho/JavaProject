# JavaProject
Project developed by 3 IUT students

### Purpose of the app
Have you ever been in situation where you want your money to be split into several categories such as for household, savings, investment, children etc.? This app has "containers" to make such stuff possible without the hassle of creating multiple credit cards and annoyance of managing and remembering everything.

Structure of the app is to be discussed and created by authors so don't mind the testing early on.
